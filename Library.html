At CBE, we understand the unique needs of international students, and we are committed to providing a supportive and
enriching environment for your academic journey. Our services for international students include:

1.Accommodation The College has a limited number of rooms in its hostels. To secure accommodation at
the College Hostels, students must complete registration then request for the same Online
(through CoSIS). After approval of the request in the system, Students are required to

report to the Dean of Students’ office to secure accommodation note. After that the studentis
given an invoice and she/he can pay the due through bank.Due to limited number of rooms at the
College Hostel, rooms are allocated to students on the first-come, first-served basis. However,
together with the first-come first-serve basis approach, priority in room allocation is given
to our international students.

2. Library The mission of the College of Business Education library is to enrich the learning,
teaching, and research journey of our college community in today’s digital world
The college’s library is a valuable resource for your academic journey. We offer
A physical library filled with different collections of materials which may be visited
from 8.00 a.m. -to 9.00 p.m. from Monday to Friday and 8.00 a.m. -to 4.00 p.m. on Saturday.
Access to e-resources through various databases. Visit https://cbe.ac.tz/library Expert assistance
from our librarian, reachable at: hlib@cbe.ac.tz

.Public buses (daladalas) and rapid transit buses (mwendokasi) provide affordable city transportation.
·Taxis, including Uber, Bolt, Taxify, and licensed taxis, are readily available at reasonable prices. ·Ferries offer a scenic way to cross the harbour.
