TTCL, or Tanzania Telecommunications Corporation, is the oldest and largest telecommunications company in Tanzania, providing a range of communication services including fixed-line and mobile telecommunications. Overview of TTCL TTCL, originally known as Tanzania Telecommunications Company Limited, was established in 1993 following the restructuring of the former Tanzania Posts and Telecommunications Corporation. It is wholly owned by the Tanzanian government and operates under the Tanzania Telecommunications Act of 1993, which designates it as the licensee for fixed basic telephone services in both mainland Tanzania and Zanzibar
